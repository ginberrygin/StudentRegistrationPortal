About Us


G'news Technologies is a technology training academy dedicated to helping students build practical coding skills and launch successful careers in software development. Through expert instruction, hands-on projects, and industry-focused learning, we prepare aspiring developers to become confident, job-ready professionals capable of solving real-world problems with technology.


Mission

To empower individuals with industry-relevant programming skills through practical, project-based learning that prepares them for successful careers in technology.

Vision

To become a leading technology academy recognized for producing highly skilled, innovative, and job-ready software developers who make meaningful contributions to the global tech industry.
